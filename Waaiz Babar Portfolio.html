3D Printing Filament Pelletizer

Designed and built a custom cutting mechanism that slices 3D printing filament into repeatable pellets. A motor and motor driver automate the cut, with the control logic programmed and simulated on an Arduino microcontroller. The electromechanical interface between the sensor logic and the cutter drives a sustainable campus recycling loop.
